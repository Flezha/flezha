flz dlq - Dead-letter queue: parked poison payloads (retry budget exhausted).

USAGE:
    flz dlq <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    list    Parked entries, nearest-to-expiry first.
    show    One entry and its payload bytes, or `nil` bytes when it carries
            none.
    replay  Re-dispatch a parked payload to its relay.
    drop    Discard a parked entry and its payload.
    help    Show this help message.

Identity for gated verbs rides `--token <token>` or the FLEZHA_TOKEN env var.
