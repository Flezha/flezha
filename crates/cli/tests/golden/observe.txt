flz observe - Observability foundation inspection.

USAGE:
    flz observe <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    sources  Every observability source registered on any controller and what it
             said recently, one row per source.
    sinks    Every controller's registered observability sinks, each with its
             `node`.
    tail     Stream the live signals of one source that the caller may see.
    enable   Enable an observability source on every controller.
    disable  Disable an observability source on every controller.
    help     Show this help message.
