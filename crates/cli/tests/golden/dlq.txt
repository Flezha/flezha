flz dlq - Dead-letter queue: parked poison payloads (retry budget exhausted).

USAGE:
    flz dlq <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    list    Every controller's parked entries, nearest-to-expiry first, each
            with the `node` holding it.
    show    One parked entry, with the `node` holding it and its payload bytes
            if it carries any.
    replay  Re-dispatch a parked payload to its relay.
    drop    Discard a parked entry and its payload.
    help    Show this help message.
