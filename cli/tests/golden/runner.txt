flz runner - Runner management commands.

USAGE:
    flz runner <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    list     Every runner serving a namespace, anywhere in the cluster.
    check    Report whether a runner on this host can reach a controller.
    drop     Drop one runner serving a namespace.
    dropped  The drop marks standing in a namespace, oldest first.
    readmit  Lift the drop mark on a runner in a namespace.
    help     Show this help message.
