# Decisions

## Awaiting the maintainer

Each entry: what it is, why it is the maintainer's call, the options **with the
cost of each**, and a recommendation. An entry with no recommendation is a question
that has not been finished. Nothing waits on an answer: the item is marked
blocked and the next one starts.

## Rulings already given

Newest first. A ruling here is work, never a question, and is not re-asked.
