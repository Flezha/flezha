flz audit - Durable audit log inspection (`flz logs ... audit`).

USAGE:
    flz audit <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    list    A kind's rotated files on every controller, each with its record
            times and digest.
    read    A kind's records from every controller, newest first, each naming
            its `node`.
    tail    Stream a kind's new records from every controller as they are
            written.
    stats   A kind's operational snapshot, one row per controller.
    rotate  Close a kind's current file and start a new one, on `node` or on
            every controller.
    help    Show this help message.
