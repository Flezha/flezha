flz observe - Observability foundation inspection.

USAGE:
    flz observe <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    sources  Every registered observability source and what it said recently.
    sinks    Every registered observability sink.
    tail     Stream the live signals of `source` that the caller may see.
    enable   Enable the source `name`.
    disable  Disable the source `name`.
    help     Show this help message.
