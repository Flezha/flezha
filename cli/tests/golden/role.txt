flz role - Principal → role bindings.

USAGE:
    flz role <COMMAND>

COMMANDS:
    bind    Bind a principal to a role in a namespace.
    unbind  Unbind a role from a principal, or the principal's whole binding.
    list    The role bindings the caller may see, one per identity.
    help    Show this help message.

A principal holds a set of roles in one namespace, and an action is allowed
when any of them grants it. A bind naming another namespace is refused.
